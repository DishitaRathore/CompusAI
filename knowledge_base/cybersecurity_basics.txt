CampusFlow AI Cybersecurity Basics

Cybersecurity means protecting systems, accounts, networks, and information from unauthorized access or misuse.

Important student security practices include:

1. Use a strong and unique password.
2. Do not share passwords or one-time passwords.
3. Enable multi-factor authentication when available.
4. Avoid clicking unknown links or downloading unexpected attachments.
5. Verify the sender before responding to an email.
6. Report suspicious messages to the institution's IT support team.
7. Keep devices, browsers, and applications updated.

If a student suspects unauthorized access, they should change their password, enable multi-factor authentication, and report the event immediately.

Source basis:
NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0 and CISA cybersecurity guidance.
